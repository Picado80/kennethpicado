01 · The challenge

An 800-capacity event venue running at a quarter of its potential. Informal management, no metrics, no documented processes, and no way to tell whether a Saturday going wrong was bad luck, bad execution or a systemic failure, because nothing was measured. The owner needed a system. Not a strategy deck, not advice: a system that would still run when the consultant left.

02 · The system

Five layers, one continuous loop.

  1. 01Policies

    What we believe and require.Internal policy portal · Service standards · SLA definitions

  2. 02Metrics

    What we measure every day.Daily occupancy · Service times · Incident counts · NPS

  3. 03Audits

    What we verify every week.15+ digital audit forms · Weekly cross-zone audits · Compliance scoring

  4. 04Dashboards

    What leaders see.6 leader-owned dashboards · Real-time KPI tracking · Trend analysis

  5. 05Weekly review

    What we discuss every Monday.Weekly business review · 6 operating domains · Action-item tracking

↺ The Monday review feeds the next round of policies. The loop is the system.

03 · What was shipped
  • Internal policy portal on Google Sites, with 18 service zones documented
  • 15+ digital audit forms covering every operational domain
  • 6 leader-owned dashboards, one per operating domain
  • 4-week structured onboarding curriculum with quizzes and weighted scoring rubrics
  • 6-week fit evaluation framework that measures cultural alignment
  • Weekly business review cadence: a Monday rhythm across 6 domains
  • Formal audit guides built on a train-the-trainer method
  • Leadership coaching program: a session series with behavioral change tracking
04 · The outcome

An 800-capacity venue went from informal to accountable in 12 weeks, without hiring an operations manager, a quality lead or an L&D lead.
