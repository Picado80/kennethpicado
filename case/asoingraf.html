01 · The challenge

An entire trade, graphic and printing professionals, watching its margins erode. Digital was eating their corporate work, AI was eating their design work and low-cost imports were eating their print premium. They didn't need motivation. They needed a new way to sell, and the association needed a campaign that could move its members in 18 days, not 18 months.
