Case 02 · Financial services

Performance evaluation platform for a 1,000-employee financial services company.

Financial services company · Shipped Q1 2025

How a hybrid 70/30 model, HRIS KPIs plus a 9-box human evaluation, was sold with an interactive mockup and then architected for engineering handoff.

01 · The challenge

A thousand employees evaluated with disconnected spreadsheets and gut feel. Oracle HRIS had the KPI data; leadership had the values and potential assessments; nothing connected the two. Reviews ended and nothing happened next: no succession pipeline, no development plans, no exits triggered. Data without consequences.

02 · The system

Seventy percent machine. Thirty percent human. One score.

Every quadrant fires its own next step. The review ends in an action, not in a file.

03 · What was shipped
  • Interactive executive mockup that sold the engagement before any code was written
  • 9-box talent grid: a values × potential evaluation rubric
  • Auto-trigger logic: succession planning, skill-building or an improvement plan, by tier
  • 3-phase implementation roadmap: pilot, scale, full rollout
  • Engineering handoff documentation for the client's technical lead
  • Pilot definitions for three departments: customer service, lending and credit, digital payments
  • Tier-based action library with the recommended actions for each quadrant
04 · The outcome

Performance evaluation became a system that triggers actions, not a spreadsheet that triggers nothing.
