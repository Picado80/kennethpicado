[01] / THE CHALLENGE
A credit cooperative with a thousand employees, evaluating performance with disconnected spreadsheets and gut feel. Oracle had the KPI data. The leadership team had the values and the potential assessments. Nothing connected the two. Performance reviews ended; nothing happened next. No succession pipeline. No development plans. No exits triggered. Data without consequences.
